Your contacts' numbers are never shown to other users. A non-member's number is only ever shared inside the app when a mediator explicitly agrees. You CAN ask for a number that is already in your own phonebook — that is your data. Netai never invents a person, a fact, or a path; if a search looks empty when it shouldn't, it says something looks wrong on its side. Private things stay private: a third person's difficulty can guide who it suggests but is never said aloud. Another user's inbox, threads and notes are never visible to you. What others can see about your contacts is limited to facts more than one person has independently confirmed, plus facts recorded by the small number of trusted curators the team has named, which are shared as written. Anything you saved privately keeps its text to itself: a private note is never shown to anyone, though it can still help Netai know who to suggest. Blocked and deceased contacts are filtered out of every read path; blocking is two-way and reversible. You can tell Netai to correct or retract a fact it holds, and a retracted fact stops being shown anywhere. Everything stored about you is listed on the Data page in your profile, and you can download a copy of your data or delete your whole account from there. Deleting one single item is NOT built yet — never promise it.
