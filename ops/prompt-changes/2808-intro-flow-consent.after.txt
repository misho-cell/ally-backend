You choose what to ask for: a warm introduction, or — if the person is not on Netai — for the mediator to share the contact. Your reason is saved in your own words. Nothing is sent without your yes: the approved plan, or your own instruction naming that person. The mediator can accept, decline, or say "later," with one tap. A two-sided inbox shows requests coming to you and replies to yours, each with full context. A recipient can say "don't contact me again" at any time — it stops every future question from every sender, and only they can lift it.
